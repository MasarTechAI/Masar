For the skeptical readerFive questions · five answers

Five questions every judge asks.
Where the answer lives on this page.

01 · Problem & solution clarity

Is the problem real, specific and validated? Does the solution address it?

Businesses have repetitive work and no clear path from “we waste hours on this” to a working AI agent. Creators can build agents but cannot find the businesses that need them. Masar is the bridge: the discovery engine turns a described workflow into an opportunity, a checked agent and a creator.

What Masar doesThe live demo
02 · Feasibility & applicability

Can this team build and run it in the Saudi market?

Phase 1 is deliberately narrow: businesses and creators around real workflows, with this prototype as the instrument. Nothing more than the discovery engine, briefs and a creator bench is needed to start. Later phases are sequenced, not promised. One revenue engine first.

Four phasesWhere creators come from
03 · Value proposition & originality

What makes this better than what users have today?

Today a business googles, asks a chatbot, compares tools and talks to developers, then often stops. Masar replaces six searches with one pathway that ends in a specific agent and a specific creator. It does not compete with models, courses or agent builders. It connects them.

Before / with MasarWhy not ChatGPT
04 · Innovation & AI adoption

Is AI core to the solution, not decoration?

AI does three jobs in the product: it interprets a described workflow, identifies the repetitive task and the bottleneck, and matches an agent with stated limitations. Every agent keeps humans in control. In this prototype the engine is transparent and rule-based, and it says so.
